mymcp privacy policy

mymcp is a personal tool used only by its owner. It runs entirely on the owner's own computer.

When the owner authorizes a Google account, the resulting access credentials are stored on that computer only. Gmail and Google Calendar data is read on demand to carry out the owner's requests and is not stored, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party by mymcp. No server operated by mymcp exists.

Use of Google user data by mymcp complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google Account security settings under Third-party apps and services.
